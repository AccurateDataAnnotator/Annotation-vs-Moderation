# Annotation vs Moderation

A practical educational guide for anyone entering the AI data industry.

This repository explains one of the most important and least discussed 
topics in AI work:

> What is the real difference between data annotation and content moderation?
> And where do they overlap?

Most job listings mix these two roles. Most beginners apply without knowing 
the difference. This guide exists to change that.

---

## What You Will Learn

- What data annotation means and what it actually involves
- What content moderation means and how it works in real platforms
- Where the two roles meet and where they separate
- What skills belong to each field
- How knowing both makes you a stronger candidate

---

## Who This Is For

- Beginners exploring remote AI data work
- Bilingual candidates looking to understand the field before applying
- Anyone who has seen both terms in job listings and felt uncertain
