# Module 02 — What is Content Moderation

## What You Will Learn

- What content moderation means in simple terms
- Why platforms need human moderators
- What a moderator actually does day to day
- How moderation differs from annotation at first glance

---

## The Simple Definition

Content moderation is the process of reviewing user generated content 
and deciding whether it follows a platform's rules or not.

Every time someone posts a comment, uploads a video, or sends a message 
on a platform, that content needs to be reviewed.

Moderation is what keeps platforms safe, legal, and usable.

---

## Why It Exists

Online platforms cannot allow everything. Some content is harmful, 
illegal, misleading, or simply against the rules of that platform.

Human moderators review that content and make decisions:

- Does this stay or get removed?
- Does this need a warning label?
- Does this need to be escalated to a specialist?

Without moderation, platforms become unsafe very quickly.

---

## What a Moderator Actually Does

Depending on the platform and project, a moderator may:

- Review a post and decide if it violates community guidelines
- Watch a video clip and flag it for violence, nudity or misinformation
- Read a comment and decide if it qualifies as hate speech
- Review a reported account and decide if it should be banned
- Apply category labels to flagged content for legal or safety teams
- Escalate urgent cases that require immediate human review

The core responsibility is always:

> Apply platform policy accurately, consistently, and quickly.

---

## Real Examples

**Social media moderation:**
A platform receives 10,000 reported posts per day. Your job is to 
review each one and decide if it violates the harassment policy.

**Video moderation:**
A streaming platform flags uploaded videos automatically. Your job 
is to review the flagged ones and confirm or reject the flag.

**Comment moderation:**
A news website asks you to review reader comments before they go live. 
Your job is to approve, edit, or remove each one based on their rules.

**Multilingual moderation:**
A global platform needs moderators who can review content in Arabic, 
French, Spanish or other languages. Your job is to apply the same 
policy across different languages accurately.

---

## Quick Summary

- Moderation means reviewing user content against platform rules
- The goal is platform safety, not AI training
- Speed and consistency under pressure are key skills
- Multilingual moderators are in high demand globally
