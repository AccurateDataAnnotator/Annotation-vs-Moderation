# Module 01 — What is Data Annotation

## What You Will Learn

- What data annotation means in simple terms

- Real examples from text, image and audio work

---

## The Simple Definition

Data annotation is the process of adding labels to raw data so that 
an AI model can learn from it.

Raw data on its own means nothing to a machine. A photo is just pixels. 
A sentence is just characters. Annotation gives that data meaning.

When you label a sentence as positive or negative, you are teaching 
the AI what human emotion looks like in text.

When you draw a box around a car in a photo, you are teaching the AI 
what a car looks like.

That is annotation.

---

## Why It Exists

AI models do not learn on their own. They learn from examples.

Those examples need to be created, checked, and labeled by humans. 
That is the entire reason the annotation industry exists.

Without human annotators, there is no AI training data. Without 
training data, there is no working AI model.

---

## What an Annotator Actually Does

Depending on the project, an annotator may:

- Read a piece of text and rate its quality
- Decide if an AI response is helpful, harmful or unclear
- Label an image with categories or bounding boxes
- Transcribe audio into written text
- Compare two AI responses and choose the better one
- Flag content that violates a guideline

Every task is different. But the core responsibility is always the same:

> Apply human judgment accurately and consistently.

---

## Real Examples

**Text annotation:**
A company building a customer service AI gives you 500 chat messages. 
Your job is to label each one as a question, complaint, or compliment.

**Preference annotation:**
An AI company shows you two responses to the same prompt. 
Your job is to choose which one is more accurate and explain why.

**Safety annotation:**
You are given a set of AI generated sentences. Your job is to flag 
any sentence that contains harmful, misleading or inappropriate content.

---

## Quick Summary

- Annotation means adding labels to data so AI can learn
- Human judgment is the core of every annotation task
- Tasks vary but accuracy and consistency are always required
- This work directly shapes how AI models behave
